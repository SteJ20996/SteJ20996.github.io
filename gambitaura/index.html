GambitAura

Small boards for two players. No dice, nothing hidden. Every move is yours.

Game 01

借路 Jielu

Two pieces each, one flag to reach. On your turn, step one square or lift a floor tile and drop it into the water. Every road you build can also be borrowed by the other side.

The whole game has been solved by computer. With the suggested opening, neither side can force a win.

Play Jielu
Open informationBoth players see everything. Same position, same options.
No luckNo dice, no shuffles, no timer. Only the moves decide.
Checked for fairnessOpenings are tested so that moving first or second gives no forced win.
