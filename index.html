01 · About

Reasoning, not just answers

I teach math, from grade-school basics to SAT Math and AP Calculus, and I am working on a doctorate in education. What interests me is not just the answer on the page but how a student got there, and whether they could do it again alone.

My research at Westcliff University asks an old question in a new situation: when an AI tool helped with the homework, how does a student decide the work is ready to hand in, and where does their own understanding fit in? Two preprints grew out of that question.

The classroom and the research share one question: how do we know what a student really understands? Everything on this page is a different angle on that problem.

Preprint · EdArXiv 2026

The Absent Cognitive Baseline

Theorizing a structural gap in AI-native college students' academic self-assessment · with Jiarui Xu

The paper suggests that when an AI tool does the thinking before a skill has formed, a student may be left with nothing of their own to compare against when they ask themselves whether they understand.

Three ways the gap shows up

  1. IUnknowabilityNot being able to tell where your own understanding ends, because there is no memory of doing this kind of work without help.
  2. IIFalse calibrationFeeling that you understand because the AI's answer reads so smoothly.
  3. IIIDe-normalization of struggleLosing the sense that getting stuck is a normal part of learning.

Preprint · EdArXiv 2026

Metacognitively Discordant Completion

The aware pass-through of non-understanding in generative AI learning

With AI, a student can hand in work that is correct and complete while knowing, at that moment, that they do not understand it. This paper gives that experience a name and works out what it is.

It takes all three at once

  1. IEffort investedReal work went into the task. This is not a student who skipped it; the effort is there in the chat history.
  2. IINon-understanding knownAt the moment of handing in, the student knows, by their own standard, that they do not understand the work.
  3. IIICompletion released anywayThey hand it in anyway, knowing that.
