01 · About

Reasoning, not just answers

I am a math educator and EdD student focused on how students actually learn, not just what they can produce on a test. My teaching spans K–8 foundations to SAT Math and AP Calculus, built on breaking complex ideas into intuitive, buildable steps.

At Westcliff University I study graphicacy, data visualization, and generative AI's impact on how the mind constructs and judges its own understanding. Both of my current papers grew out of that line of work.

The classroom and the research share one question: how do we know what a student really understands? Everything on this page is a different angle on that problem.
