01 · About

Reasoning, not just answers

I teach math, from grade-school basics to SAT Math and AP Calculus, and I am working on a doctorate in education. What interests me is not just the answer on the page but how a student got there, and whether they could do it again alone.

My research at Westcliff University asks an old question in a new situation: when an AI tool helped with the homework, how does a student decide the work is ready to hand in, and where does their own understanding fit in? Two preprints grew out of that question.

The classroom and the research share one question: how do we know what a student really understands? Everything on this page is a different angle on that problem.
