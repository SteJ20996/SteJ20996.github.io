Metacognitively Discordant Completion and the Aware Pass‑Through of Non‑Understanding in Generative AI Learning

Yizhen Jia · College of Education, Westcliff University

01 · Abstract

Generative AI lets a learner submit correct, complete work while knowing that understanding has not arrived, a state this paper names. Metacognitively discordant completion (MDC) is the experiential state of a learner who has invested cognitive effort in an academic task, who holds a formed, first-person awareness that understanding has not occurred, judged against the learner's own operative standard, and who releases the completion anyway. The conjunction of the three conditions is the construct, and the paper argues that no existing literature holds the three together under one name.

The argument proceeds by inheritance and by boundary. Metacognition research supplies the verdict machinery and the judgment economy of answer release. A pre-AI genealogy across five literatures establishes that the dissociation between completing and understanding is old. Four cuts separate the construct from withdrawn effort, justification discourse, fluency illusions, and preprint-stage constructs. A sibling construct, the Absent Cognitive Baseline, marks the case where no verdict can form, and frameworks from epistemic akrasia to academic dishonesty are each engaged on the one element they lack.

The technology's part is amplification, not invention, a framing credited to the field. Evidence questions are assigned to a planned interpretative phenomenological study with graphicacy as the anchoring domain.

02 · The Three Conditions

All three, together, are MDC

  • IEffort investedReal cognitive work went into the task. The evening's effort is visible in the chat log.
  • IINon-understanding knownA formed, first-person verdict that understanding has not occurred, judged by the learner's own standard.
  • IIICompletion released anywayThe work is submitted with that verdict intact.

03 · What MDC Is Not

Three families, three misses

The illusion family says the learner believes understanding happened, yet here the learner already has the truth. The integrity family says a rule was broken, yet no rule was broken. The disengagement family says effort was withdrawn, yet the effort is real. MDC is the state all three frames miss.
