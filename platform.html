01 · What it is

Lessons, checks, and projects

Each course has short lessons, a few check problems with several parts, and two field projects set in real-life situations. Quidence checks the fixed parts of your answers and saves your calculations, drawings, and explanations for you to review. It does not check every written step on its own; the report is something to talk over with a teacher or tutor.

02 · Subjects

Ten courses in three groups

  • School MathematicsPre-Algebra, Algebra I, Geometry, Algebra II
  • Further MathematicsPre-calculus, Calculus, Statistics, Linear Algebra
  • SciencePhysics 1 Foundations, Physics 2 Foundations

03 · Your work stays with you

Saved in your browser, not on a server

New work is saved in your own browser. Accounts and cloud sync are paused during public testing, so download a copy of your records before you leave a shared device.
