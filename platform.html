Research Prototype · Working Name: Delta

Learning that closes the gap.

Not an AI that answers your questions, but an AI that figures out how you actually understand, from what you do, not what you say.

→ Try the live demo

01 · The Gap

You are a poor judge of your own understanding

Learners are systematically miscalibrated: we routinely feel we've understood something we haven't. Psychologists call this metacognitive miscalibration, and it's the single biggest thing today's AI tutors miss. They answer the question you thought to ask, and you can't request help with a gap you can't see.

02 · The Core

Effort is the mechanism, not the price

Four decades of research on desirable difficulties (Bjork) show that methods which feel effortful (retrieval, spacing, interleaving) produce the most durable learning, while smooth ones produce almost none. Every effortful act here does double duty: it builds stronger memory, and it emits the behavioral data Delta needs to model you accurately.

03 · The Honest Part

What "adaptive" does, and doesn't, mean

Fixed learning styles (visual, auditory, kinesthetic) have been repeatedly tested and found not to hold up, so Delta never guesses what kind of person you are. It adapts to something measurable instead: your understanding state, right now, on this specific idea, inferred from evidence and updated every step.

    05 · How It Works

    A four-step loop that never stops

    • 01Effortful diagnosisYou attempt, commit, and predict your confidence, before any hint.
    • 02Build the modelYour behavior updates a live learner model: shaky ideas, misconceptions, decay.
    • 03Adapt the next stepA harder variant, a scaffold, a new representation, or a spaced re-test.
    • 04Hold up the mirrorDelta shows you what it inferred; seeing the model is itself the training.

    06 · What It Adapts On

    Four evidence-based dimensions

    • ↑↓ ZPDDifficultyEvery item sits just past what you can already do alone.
    • ▿ DIAGNOSTICMisconceptionsWrong answers encode which broken mental model produced them.
    • ∞ FORGETTING CURVESSpacingEach idea returns right as you're about to forget it.
    • ∑ REPRESENTATIONSRepresentationSymbolic, visual, numeric, worked example: it remembers which one unlocks you.

    07 · The Contract

    An honest deal with the learner

    It asks

    • Attempt before you're told, every time
    • Commit instead of staying vague
    • Predict your confidence, honestly
    • Explain your reasoning
    • Return for the spaced re-tests

    It gives

    • A map of what you actually know
    • Time spent only on shaky ideas
    • Learning that survives past the test
    • Metacognition: you learn to see your gaps
    • A tutor that adapts to you, not the average

    08 · Coming Next

    Your own homework is the best question bank

    The demo uses curated problems; the real version starts from a phone photo of tonight's worksheet, messy handwriting included. A vision-capable Claude model extracts each problem and drafts its misconception map, then the mirror runs on every one: your answer, your reasoning, your certainty, the full report.

    The inversion is the whole point: photo-solver apps use your homework to hand you answers; Delta uses the same photo to map your understanding. It ships with Phase 1, because there is no honest rule-based stand-in for reading a photo.
