Graphicacy in the Generative AI Era: A PRISMA‑ScR Scoping Review of an Emerging Three‑Way Intersection in Education

Yizhen Jia & Jiarui Xu · College of Education, Westcliff University

01 · The Question

Where three things meet

This review looks for studies where three things meet: reading or making charts, generative AI, and learning. The skill of reading and making charts is called graphicacy.

It asks how much research sits at that meeting point, what job the AI does there, and how the studies measure chart skills.

The plan was fixed and registered before screening began. A registered plan like this is called a protocol.
